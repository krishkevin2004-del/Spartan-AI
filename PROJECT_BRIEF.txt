WILSON HALL HANDBOOK ASSISTANT — PROJECT BRIEF
Krish Gupta, Resident Assistant, Wilson Hall, MSU (2023-2026)


THE PROBLEM

Residents have small questions, guests, quiet hours, lockouts, room changes,
that don't feel worth troubling their RA for. A lot of them are simple
yes-or-no problems that come up any time, day or night, and the honest
answer still lives in a PDF nobody reads. So it lands on RAs, front desk
staff, and hall directors instead.


THE IDEA

A simple web assistant that answers policy questions in seconds and shows
the exact handbook section it used. It only answers from official REHS
documents. When it's unsure, or the topic is sensitive, it hands the
student to their RA, the hall office, or the right emergency number,
instead of guessing.


HOW IT WORKS (Retrieval-Augmented Generation, plain terms)

The model has to find the answer in the handbook before it's allowed to
speak. If it can't find it, it says so instead of guessing.

1. Ask      — a resident types a question, in plain language, any time.
2. Retrieve — the system searches the official handbook and pulls only the
              exact passages that match.
3. Answer   — the model drafts a reply using only those passages, and
              cites the section.
4. Escalate — no match, or a sensitive topic, and it hands off to a person
              immediately, no model involved in that decision.


THE NUMBERS (conservative planning estimates, not promises)

Wilson Hall (v1 pilot):
  1,200 residents × 25% ask a question a month × 2 min average per question
  = 10 hours of staff time back every month.

MSU-wide (28 residence halls, ~15,000 residents), same assumptions:
  = roughly 125 hours back every month.

Not every resident asks, and not every question takes the same time, these
are deliberately conservative. Running cost during build and testing: about
$15 a month. The pilot replaces all of this with a measured baseline.


BUILT TO BE SAFE — THE GUARDRAILS

- No student data. No logins, no personal info, no access to housing
  systems. Anonymous by design, keeps privacy review simple.
- Approved sources only. Answers come only from REHS documents, with the
  section cited. Off-topic questions are refused. Rate limits stop abuse.
- Zero-creativity RAG. Model temperature locked to 0.0. If the answer isn't
  in the retrieved passages, it says so, it never guesses or fills in from
  general knowledge.
- Hardcoded escalation. Self-harm, safety, conduct, and roommate-conflict
  topics never reach the model's judgment. A keyword list plus a separate
  safety-classifier call run before generation, and anything uncertain
  defaults to escalating, not to a generic "I didn't understand." Routes
  straight to a fixed, unchangeable screen with real resources (988, MSU
  CAPS, Crisis Text Line, MSU Police) and a click-to-call. The model
  cannot improvise its way around this.
- Sensitive topics go to a person. Conduct, safety, or roommate conflicts
  are handed off immediately to an RA, the hall office, or the right
  emergency number.
- Sandboxed scope. v1 is Wilson Hall only, a contained blast radius before
  anyone discusses campus-wide.
- Tested before launch. 50 to 100 real questions with known answers, plus a
  stress test to try to break it, before any resident sees it.


HOW WE'LL KNOW IT WORKED

- 90%+ accuracy on the test set, counting correct answers or proper
  hand-offs, not just "sounds plausible."
- Baseline comparison: questions the assistant handles vs. what used to
  reach staff.
- Thumbs up/down from residents on every answer.
- Actual monthly cost vs. the ~$15 planning number.


IF THIS WORKS, WHAT IT ADDS UP TO

- RA Burnout Protection — low-stakes volume stops crowding out attention
  for the moments that actually matter.
- Staff Time Back — hours returned to community building, not repeating
  the handbook.
- Early Policy Tackling — common, confusing policies get flagged and
  clarified before they cause real disputes.
- Consistent, Cited Answers — every resident gets the same accurate
  answer, day or night, hall to hall.


TECH STACK (v1)

- Model: Claude API, Claude Haiku 4.5
- RAG over the current MSU on-campus housing handbook PDF (chunk + embed
  the handbook, vector search for retrieval)
- Frontend: simple web app, hosted free on Vercel
- No accounts, no persistent per-resident data
